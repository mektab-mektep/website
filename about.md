Maktab-Mektep is a school that brings together research and practice. We promote ecocritical architecture in Central Asia through collective action.

The school is grounded in the principles of environmental justice, inclusion, and the decentralization of knowledge. That is why we organize our programs beyond Almaty, Astana, and Tashkent. We focus on non-capital cities, where the effects of environmental crises and social inequality are particularly acute.

The first edition of the school took place in Aktau in June 2026. We are now preparing new gatherings in other cities across the region.
