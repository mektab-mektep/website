COL1:
Participants from different parts of the world — from Central Asia to South America — formed the 2026 research group. It brought together young professionals from a wide range of fields, including architecture, contemporary art, natural sciences, media, and urban activism. Together, we explored the history and present-day realities of Aktau and the Mangystau region — largely through encounters with representatives of local scientific, cultural, civic, and entrepreneurial communities.
